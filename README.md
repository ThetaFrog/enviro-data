# enviro-data

_**Brief**_:

Enviro-data is an ongoing personal data analysis project focused on energy, environmental science and policy. The project uses a two-pronged approach: exploring high-level energy/environmental concepts downward into the data, while simultaneously aggregating raw data to uncover bottom-up insights. The repository is organised into distinct, topic-focused modules containing data pipelines, visualisations, and written analyses.

Like many personal projects, enviro-data is in constant flux. My own questions and research as well as other people's projects regularly draw me towards new areas. As such, the purposes and lines of inquiry will mutate over time. In addition, the extracurricular nature of the work means that there's much more that I want to do and am actively researching than I have been able to put out. Slowly, though, I hope to bring more visualisations/analyses online.


_**Lines of Inquiry**_:
* **Explore empirical data to broaden understanding of natural and anthropogenic phenomena**: things like energy production data, land use by geography etc. are important to know in themselves, but also act as metrics which can be applied to higher-level analysis and towards understanding the changing relationship between ecology and economy.
* **Phenomenological synthesis:** Combined insights from multiple data sources can be used to build deeper understandings of larger, more complex phenomena.
* **Policy and Trend Analysis:** longitudinal data from multiple sources can be used to gain an understanding of how company or governmental policy is helping or hurting the environment in the long term.


**_Purposes_**:
* **Technical (Personal)**: Learn about processing, cleaning, presenting and analysing data using Python.
* **Impact**: Present otherwise difficult to interpret data in clear and interactive ways to raise awareness for environmental issues and champion the energy transition
* **Professional**: Build a public portfolio demonstrating competence in data engineering, statistical analysis, and data storytelling.
